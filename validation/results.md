## Docking coverage after Open Babel receptor prep

`MeekoReceptorPreparer` is no longer the default. `OpenBabelReceptorPreparer`
(`obabel -ipdb -opdbqt -xr`) is, because it produced a non-empty PDBQT for
every wild-type and mutant receptor in the matrix (ABL1 5HU9, ALK 4Z55,
KIT 8PQD, BRAF 8C7X). Meeko still lives in the codebase for comparison.

**This is more cases scored, not a more trustworthy binding-affinity claim.**
Four of nine gold-standard drug-pairs now produce a Vina delta:

| Case | Drug | Delta (kcal/mol) | Why this is now possible |
|---|---|---|---|
| EGFR T790M | gefitinib | +0.591 | already in contact range; default prep no longer meeko-dependent |
| ABL1 T315I | imatinib | +3.230 | previously blocked on mutant receptor prep |
| ALK G1202R | crizotinib | +1.854 | previously blocked on receptor prep |
| ALK I1171T | crizotinib | −0.005 | previously blocked on receptor prep |

The other five are still unscored, for reasons Open Babel does not address:

- EGFR T790M / osimertinib and EGFR C797S / osimertinib — covalent mechanism gate (Vina cannot represent the covalent bond).
- KIT D816V, KIT V560G, BRAF V600E — mutation is beyond the 8 Å docking contact range.

The pre-committed 70% directionality bar is still **not met** (2/9). Published
Vina error is 1.0–2.19 kcal/mol; several of these deltas sit inside or next to
that bar. Do not report this run as evidence the binding-affinity prediction
itself is more accurate.
